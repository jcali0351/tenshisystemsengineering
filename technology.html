    Japanese Robotics Patent Explorer

    Japan is one of the world's great homes of robotics, and its patents are a map of where the field is heading. Search live records of patents published in Japan, explore twelve robotics fields or look up the country's leading robot makers. Every search runs on public patent databases, so the results are always current.

    Robotics fields

    • 01 Industrial robot arms Articulated arms for welding, painting, assembly and handling. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 02 Robot hands and grippers Fingers, suction cups and end effectors that grasp objects. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 03 Collaborative robots Cobots designed to work safely beside people. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 04 Humanoid robots Robots with a human form, from research to reception. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 05 Legged robots Walking, running and balancing machines. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 06 Surgical robots Precision systems that assist surgeons in the operating room. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 07 Care and assistive robots Robots that help with nursing care and daily life. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 08 Exoskeletons Wearable power-assist suits that support the body. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 09 Robot vision Cameras and sensing that let robots see and measure. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 10 Motion control Controllers, servos and reducers that move robots precisely. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 11 Autonomous mobile robots Robots that find their own way through factories and buildings. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
    • 12 Robot learning and AI Machine learning that teaches robots new skills. Search patents ↗ ((opens in a new tab))
