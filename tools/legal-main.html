2. Data Minimization: We Only Collect What Helps You Build

We believe you shouldn't have to surrender your digital privacy just to use a helpful online tool. We do not build user profiles, track your browsing history across the web, or collect personal details irrelevant to the tool's core function. Specifically, any information collected falls strictly into three functional categories:

  • Build & Configuration Inputs: Any parameters, text, settings, or code inputs you enter into the tool are processed solely to assemble and deliver your requested build or output.
  • Optional Contact Information (Email): Providing your email address is only required if you explicitly choose to have your build results emailed to you, save a project link, or contact us for technical support.
  • Essential Diagnostic Telemetry: To keep the tool fast and reliable, our servers automatically log basic, non-identifying technical metadata (such as HTTP status codes, build processing times, error logs, and anonymized aggregate traffic counts). This data is used exclusively to diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve build performance.

4. Data Retention & Your Control

Because we only collect data to assist with your build, we do not hold onto it longer than necessary:
