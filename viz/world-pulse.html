Data sources

Aircraft: adsb.lol (ODbL), live. Ships and trains: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi (CC BY 4.0), live for Finland and the Baltic. Data centres and cables: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Airports: OurAirports (public domain). Routes: OpenFlights (ODbL), a 2014 snapshot. Land, ports and railways: Natural Earth (public domain). Population: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Markets: Yahoo Finance, delayed. Births and deaths: modelled from World Bank Open Data rates (CC BY 4.0); each spark is a statistically expected event, not a record of a person. Road counts: Fintraffic (Finland). Day and night: worked out from the time.
