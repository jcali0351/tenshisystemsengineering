Data sources

Aircraft: adsb.lol (ODbL), live. Ships and trains: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi (CC BY 4.0), live for Finland and the Baltic. Data centres and cables: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Military bases: also OpenStreetMap, the sites volunteers have mapped as bases, airfields, naval bases, barracks, ranges and training areas; not a complete list. Airports: OurAirports (public domain). Routes: OpenFlights (ODbL), a 2014 snapshot. Land, ports and railways: Natural Earth (public domain). Precipitation: NASA GPM IMERG through NASA GIBS, a satellite estimate a few hours behind real time. Clouds: VIIRS / MODIS corrected reflectance through NASA GIBS, yesterday's passes; bright, colourless pixels are taken as cloud. Waves and surf reports: NOAA GFS-Wave model (the open-ocean swell beside each break, not the breaking wave) and NOAA NDBC wave buoys, observed. Ocean currents: NOAA CoastWatch geostrophic currents, worked out from satellite sea-level measurements. Tides: NOAA CO-OPS predictions for US stations; the level between high and low water is worked out in your browser. Wind, jet stream and temperature: NOAA GFS weather model (public domain), a calculated picture at about 100 km resolution, refreshed every few hours. Earthquakes: USGS, live. Wildfires, storms and volcanoes: NASA EONET. Aurora: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center. Satellites: orbital elements from CelesTrak, moved with a simplified orbit model. Space station: wheretheiss.at. Power stations: WRI Global Power Plant Database (CC BY 4.0). Population: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Markets: Yahoo Finance, delayed; open or closed is worked out from each exchange's regular hours, without public holidays. Satellites above you and space station passes: worked out in your browser from the same orbits, as estimates. Births and deaths: modelled from World Bank Open Data rates (CC BY 4.0); each spark is a statistically expected event, not a record of a person. Road counts: Fintraffic (Finland). Day and night: worked out from the time.

Not for navigation or trading.
