    Master Baiter Pro

    One-key fishing with live auction house prices and a leaderboard for every angler.

    Master Baiter Pro

    World of Warcraft add-on

    Master Baiter Pro turns fishing into one key and every fish into a number. Press the key to cast, press it again when the bobber splashes, and the catch pops up with its icon and auction house price while a gold counter rolls up your haul.

    • Free
    • Version 2.3.1
    • Open it in game with /mbp
    Download MasterBaiterPro.zip Get it on CurseForge (Midnight, (opens in a new tab))

    Features

    • 01

      One key fishing

      The same key casts and hooks. Bind it once and fishing becomes a rhythm instead of a chore.

    • 02

      Every fish, priced

      It looks up the auction house price of everything you catch (or uses Auctionator or TSM), and shows your haul and gold per hour as you fish.

    • 03

      Your top five

      Your five most valuable catches sit on the panel as their item icons, with prices on hover, for the session or for all time.

    • 04

      A live leaderboard

      Every copy finds the others, shows who is fishing right now and ranks everyone by catches, best catch, this week and gold per hour.

    • 05

      Records and jackpots

      Streaks, landing rate, personal records and an alert when something rare or valuable takes the bait.

    • 06

      Two editions

      Master Baiter Pro for Midnight, and Master Bait Forever for World of Warcraft: Forever, with its own leaderboard.

    How to install

    1. 01

      The easy way: install it with the CurseForge app, which keeps it up to date for you.

    2. 02

      Or download the .zip for your edition and unzip it.

    3. 03

      Move the folder into World of Warcraft › _retail_ › Interface › AddOns (use _forever_ for the Forever edition).

    4. 04

      Restart the game or type /reload, then open it with its command.

    Plays fair

    It is a helper, not a bot: every cast and every hook starts with your own key press. It never moves your character and never reads the game's memory.
