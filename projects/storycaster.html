StoryCaster

World of Warcraft add-on

StoryCaster turns a story, a speech or a whole scene into /emote lines that play out on a timer while your character acts it out: a bow, a wave, a laugh, a kneel. Your words, your timing, and no typing in the middle of the moment.

  • Free
  • Version 2.0.1
  • Open it in game with /story
Download StoryCaster.zip Get it on CurseForge (Midnight, (opens in a new tab))

Features

  • 01

    Your words, performed

    Paste your roleplay and it becomes emotes on a timer, or one line at a time with a single Deliver key, with 18 animation buttons and tags like [bow] inside the text.

  • 02

    The Lore Library

    Over 60 ready-made sets of 50+ lines for every class, race, faction, profession, holiday and occasion, ready to perform in one click.

  • 03

    Duets, scenes and choices

    Split a script into parts for two or more players and keep them in sync, break it into scenes, set the mood and write choose-your-path branches.

  • 04

    Stage themes

    Pirate (701 lines and a pirate accent), Love, War, Peace, quick Dungeon group phrases and a cheeky but never explicit After Dark tab behind an age check.

  • 05

    Player Focus and Ideas

    Weave another player into your lines now and then, and read their Total RP 3 or MyRolePlay profile for openers and prompts.

  • 06

    Made to be comfortable

    A mini player with Studio, Library and Stage panels that slide out underneath, compact mode, key bindings, backup and restore and a minimap button.

How to install

  1. 01

    The easy way: install it with the CurseForge app, which keeps it up to date for you.

  2. 02

    Or download the .zip for your edition and unzip it.

  3. 03

    Move the folder into World of Warcraft › _retail_ › Interface › AddOns (use _forever_ for the Forever edition).

  4. 04

    Restart the game or type /reload, then open it with its command.

Plays fair

StoryCaster never posts faster than one line every 15 seconds or four a minute, so your roleplay stays within the game's chat rules.
