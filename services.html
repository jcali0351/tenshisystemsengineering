Every service in detail

What each service covers, how we approach it, and what you can expect from working with us.

Software Development

Custom software built for reliability, performance, and long-term support, from first prototype to production.

Software Development is where ideas become working tools. We build custom business applications, web and desktop programs, internal dashboards and the connections that let separate systems share data, from a first clickable prototype through to a stable release. Before writing code we map how your team really works, which steps waste time, and what the software must never get wrong, so the finished product fits your business instead of forcing your business to fit it.

We write clean, tested and documented code, use version control and code reviews on every project, and plan releases so updates never surprise your users. When older software is holding you back, we modernize it step by step without stopping daily operations. After launch we stay on as your maintenance partner, fixing issues quickly, keeping dependencies secure and adding features as your needs grow.

  • Custom business applications
  • Web and desktop applications
  • API and system integration
  • Modernizing older software
  • Testing, documentation, and long-term maintenance
MORE: Software Development

Technology Consulting

Strategic guidance for digital transformation, modernization, and emerging technologies.

Technology Consulting gives you an experienced, independent view before you spend money. We review your current systems, infrastructure, software licences and workflows, talk with the people who use them every day, and identify what is working, what is risky and what is costing more than it should. You receive a clear written assessment in plain language, not jargon.

From there we build a practical roadmap with priorities, timelines and realistic budgets: which systems to keep, which to modernize, and where new technology such as cloud services or AI would genuinely help. We can compare vendors objectively, review proposals from other suppliers, and stay involved as your project guide, making sure the plan is delivered as agreed and that your team is confident with every change.

  • IT strategy and roadmaps
  • Systems and architecture reviews
  • Digital transformation planning
  • Vendor and technology selection
  • Project guidance and oversight
MORE: Technology Consulting

Media Design

Brand identity, websites, video, and digital media designed to communicate clearly and leave a lasting impression.

Media Design turns your message into visuals people notice and understand. We create graphics for print and screens, presentations and company documents, social media artwork, edited video, motion graphics and carefully retouched photography. Every piece starts with a short brief about who will see it, where they will see it and what you want them to do next.

Our designers work with your brand colours, fonts and tone so everything you publish feels like it comes from the same company. We deliver files in the right formats and sizes for each channel, from printed brochures to vertical video for phones, and keep editable source files so future updates are quick. Whether you need a single launch video or a steady stream of campaign material, we keep quality and deadlines consistent.

  • Graphic design for print and digital
  • Presentation and document design
  • Video editing and motion graphics
  • Photo direction and image editing
  • Social media graphics
MORE: Media Design

Web Design

Clean, responsive websites designed around your brand and your visitors, on every screen.

Web Design shapes the experience visitors have the moment they arrive on your site. We start by understanding your audience and their goals, then plan the structure of every page, sketch wireframes and build interactive prototypes you can click through before anything is developed. This lets you test ideas early and change direction cheaply.

The finished designs are responsive, so they look and work beautifully on phones, tablets and large screens. We design for accessibility from the start, with readable type, strong colour contrast and clear navigation, so everyone can use your website comfortably. For existing sites we audit what visitors struggle with and redesign around those findings, keeping the content and search rankings you have already earned.

  • Responsive layouts for every screen
  • Wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • User experience (UX) planning
  • Accessible, easy-to-read design
  • Redesigns of existing websites
MORE: Web Design

Web Development

Fast, secure websites and web applications built on modern, maintainable code.

Web Development turns designs into fast, secure and reliable websites and web applications. We write standards-based HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and use modern frameworks such as React, Vue and Next.js when a project calls for rich interactivity: customer portals, booking systems, online stores and tools that connect to your existing databases and services.

Performance is built in from the start: optimized images, efficient code and careful loading so pages appear quickly even on slow mobile connections, which also helps your search engine ranking. We follow security best practices, test across browsers and devices, and support multiple languages, including right-to-left scripts. After launch we provide updates, monitoring and improvements so your site stays fast and safe for years.

  • Front-end development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • Web applications and customer portals
  • Online stores and booking systems
  • Speed and search engine optimization
  • Security updates and ongoing support
MORE: Web Development

Hosting

Reliable hosting, domains, SSL, and backups, monitored and maintained so your site stays online.

Hosting covers everything needed to keep your website and applications online, secure and quick to load. We choose the right environment for your project, from simple static hosting to cloud servers and global content delivery networks, register and manage your domains and DNS, and install SSL certificates so every visit is encrypted.

Your data is protected with automatic backups and a tested recovery plan, and we monitor uptime around the clock so problems are spotted and fixed early, often before visitors notice. We apply security updates, renew certificates and domains on time, and send you clear reports. You get one reliable contact for all of it instead of juggling several providers and their renewal notices.

  • Website and application hosting
  • Domain registration and DNS management
  • SSL certificates and security
  • Automatic backups and recovery
  • Uptime monitoring and maintenance
MORE: Hosting

AI Services

Practical AI solutions, from chat assistants to automation, that save time and support your team.

AI Services help your team use artificial intelligence where it truly saves time. We start with a short review of your daily work to find repetitive tasks such as answering common questions, searching long documents, summarizing reports or copying data between systems, and estimate honestly where AI will help and where a simpler solution is better.

We then build and connect practical tools: chat assistants for your website or support team, secure search across your own documents, and automated workflows linked to the software you already use. Every solution is tested for accuracy, keeps sensitive data protected and includes human review where decisions matter. We also train your staff and set clear guidelines so AI is used responsibly and with confidence.

  • Chat assistants for websites and support
  • Document search and summaries
  • Workflow and data-entry automation
  • AI connected to your existing systems
  • Guidance on responsible AI use
MORE: AI Services

Content Management

Easy-to-use content management so your team can update pages, news, and media without code.

Content Management puts your website in your own hands. We set up and configure systems such as WordPress, Strapi or Shopify so your team can update pages, news, product listings and images through a simple editor, without touching code or waiting for a developer to be free.

We build custom page templates and reusable content blocks that keep every page on brand, migrate your existing content carefully, and organise multilingual sites so Japanese, English and other versions stay in step. Each person gets the right permissions for their role, and we provide hands-on training and a written editing guide so new staff can learn quickly.

  • CMS setup and configuration
  • Custom page templates and content blocks
  • Moving content from your current website
  • Multilingual content (Japanese and English)
  • Staff training and editing guides
MORE: Content Management

Branding

Logos, color, typography, and brand guidelines that give your business a clear, consistent identity.

Branding gives your business a clear, memorable identity. We begin by learning your story, values, customers and competitors, then develop a visual direction that sets you apart: logo design or refresh, colour palettes, typography and the small details that make a brand recognisable at a glance.

Everything is documented in practical brand guidelines that explain how to use your logo, colours and fonts correctly in print and online. We then apply the identity to business cards, stationery, signage, presentation and document templates, and social media graphics, so your company looks consistent and professional wherever customers meet it, from a website to a trade show booth.

  • Logo design and refresh
  • Color palettes and typography
  • Brand guidelines
  • Business cards and stationery
  • Templates for documents and social media
