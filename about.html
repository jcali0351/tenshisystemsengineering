About us

An engineering company in Osaka, building dependable technology with care.

Who we are

Tenshi Systems Engineering is an engineering and technology company based in Osaka, Japan. We help organizations plan, build and run the systems they depend on, from custom software and websites to hosting, AI and branding.

We believe good technology should be dependable, easy to understand and built to last. That is why we take time to listen, explain our choices clearly and stay involved long after launch.

天使

Our name

Tenshi (天使) means “angel” in Japanese. For us it is a promise: to look after every project, and every client, with care, from the first conversation to long after launch.

What we value

  • Reliability

    Systems that keep working, built on proven methods and careful testing.

  • Craftsmanship

    Attention to detail in every line of code, every layout and every handover.

  • Partnership

    Together Best Cooperation: we work alongside your team, not just for it.

  • Clarity

    Plain explanations, honest advice and no unnecessary jargon.

Based in Osaka

Osaka is a city of makers and merchants, known for practical thinking and warm hospitality. We bring that same spirit to every project.
