About us

An engineering company in Osaka, building dependable technology with care.

Who we are

Tenshi Systems Engineering is an engineering and technology company based in Osaka, Japan. We help organizations plan, build and run the systems they depend on, from custom software and websites to hosting, AI and branding.

We believe good technology should be dependable, easy to understand and built to last. That is why we take time to listen, explain our choices clearly and stay involved long after launch.

天使

Our name

Tenshi (天使) means “angel” in Japanese. For us it is a promise: to look after every project, and every client, with care, from the first conversation to long after launch.

Our mission

To design, build and host websites that people can rely on: fast, secure, accessible and easy to manage, launched with care and looked after for as long as they are online.

Our vision

A web where every business, from a family shop in Osaka to a company working across continents, has a website that is well made, honest and quick for everyone who visits it.

Our speciality: the web

We offer nine services, but the web is where we spend most of our days. Design, development and hosting are three halves of the same job to us: a beautiful site is useless if it is slow, and a fast site is useless if it goes offline. We handle all three, so nothing falls between the cracks.

Web design

Design starts with your visitors, not with colours. We study who they are, what they came to do and what stops them, then plan the structure of every page as a sitemap and wireframes. Before any code is written you click through an interactive prototype on your own phone and computer, so ideas are tested early and changes cost little.

Every layout is designed mobile-first and adapts smoothly to tablets and large screens. We build a small design system for your site, with type scale, colours, spacing, buttons and form styles, so every new page looks like it belongs. Accessibility is part of the design from the beginning, not a fix at the end.

  • Visitor research, sitemaps and wireframes
  • Interactive prototypes in Figma
  • Mobile-first, responsive layouts
  • Readable type, strong contrast and clear navigation

Web development

We turn designs into clean, standards-based HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Simple sites stay simple and static, which makes them fast and very secure; richer projects such as portals, online stores and booking systems use modern frameworks like React, Vue or Next.js, connected to the databases and services you already use.

Speed is planned, not hoped for. We set a performance budget, compress and resize every image, load only the code a page needs and test against Google's Core Web Vitals, because fast pages keep visitors and rank better in search. Every site is tested in the major browsers and on real devices, supports multiple languages including right-to-left scripts, and can be edited through a content management system if you want one.

  • Semantic, standards-based code
  • Performance budgets and Core Web Vitals
  • Multilingual and right-to-left support
  • Content management for your team

Hosting and care

We choose the right home for every site: fast static hosting on a global content delivery network for most websites, and managed cloud servers for applications that need them. We register and manage domains and DNS, install SSL certificates so every page loads over HTTPS, and configure security headers and firewall rules.

Your site is backed up automatically, and we test that the backups really restore. Uptime monitoring watches the site around the clock and alerts us the moment something goes wrong. We keep software and certificates up to date, renew domains on time and send you a clear report, so you always know your site is healthy without having to think about it.

  • Global CDN and managed cloud hosting
  • Domains, DNS and SSL certificates
  • Automatic, tested backups
  • 24/7 uptime monitoring and updates

From idea to live website

A typical website project moves through six stages. You always know which stage we are in and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A conversation about your goals, your visitors and your budget, followed by a written proposal in plain language.

  2. 02

    Design

    Sitemap, wireframes and an interactive prototype you can try on your own devices before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Build

    Clean code, real content and regular previews on a private test site so you can follow the progress.

  4. 04

    Test

    Checks on phones, tablets and computers, in every major browser, for speed, accessibility, security and every form.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Domain, SSL, hosting and search engine setup, then a careful switch-over with no downtime for your visitors.

  6. 06

    Care

    Monitoring, backups, updates and improvements for as long as your site is online, with a real person to call.

Our standards
