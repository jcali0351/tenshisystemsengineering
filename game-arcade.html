    About Neon Arcade

    Defend the neon city. Click (or tap) anywhere in the sky to fire: drones, saucers, cubes and shooting stars are worth points, combos multiply them, and every so often a mothership, a kaiju or a giant robot turns up to eat the skyline. Keep your shields up as long as you can.

    Controls

    • Click or tap the sky to shoot; hold for rapid fire when you have it
    • Weapons: the bar on the left, or keys 1 to 6
    • Power-ups: R rapid fire, S spread shot, T slow time, N nuke
    • Shoot the falling bombs before they reach the city
    • Secret: the Konami code turns on INSANE MODE
